# 플레이어 캐릭터 (모험가) — 제미나이 캐릭터 시트 프롬프트

- 용도: 플레이어 캐릭터를 모험가 모양으로 다시 디자인한다. 3D 모델의 참고 이미지(블렌더 · AI 이미지→3D)로 쓴다.
- 비율은 앱에 넣은 B 비율에 맞췄다 (약 2.5등신: 머리 1.8배, 다리 0.68배). 경희몬 공통 스타일 (`05_Characters/제미나이_프롬프트_단체시트.txt`) 과 같은 토이 느낌.
- 프롬프트 1-B = 한 캐릭터의 전체 시트 (대표 전신샷 + 앞 · 뒤 · 왼쪽 · 오른쪽 + 표정 6개). **이걸 먼저 쓴다.** 남 · 여 따로 한 장씩.
- 프롬프트 1 = 남녀를 한 장에 놓고 비교하는 간단 턴어라운드 (앞 · 45도 · 오른쪽 · 뒤).
- 프롬프트 2 = 3D 변환용 정면 한 장 (시트에서 마음에 드는 쪽을 골라 다시 뽑기).

## 프롬프트 1-B — 전체 캐릭터 시트 (전신샷 · 4방향 · 표정)

`[MALE / FEMALE]` 은 하나만 남기고, 그 줄에 맞는 머리 모양 한 줄만 남긴다. 남 · 여 각각 한 번씩 뽑는다.

```
Create ONE single image: a complete professional character design sheet for ONE mobile game player character,
a young [MALE / FEMALE] university student dressed as a cheerful campus adventurer.
Plain flat light cream background (#FAF6EC), horizontal 16:9, clean organized layout with generous spacing, nothing overlapping.
No text, labels, numbers, arrows, grid lines, frames or logos anywhere.

LAYOUT (three areas)
1. LEFT (about one third of the width): one large full-body hero shot, three-quarter front view,
   a lively happy pose (one hand waving, the other holding the satchel strap, a small step forward), big smile.
2. TOP RIGHT: a turnaround row of the SAME character in 4 full-body views, left to right:
   FRONT view, LEFT side view (character facing left), RIGHT side view (character facing right), BACK view.
   All 4 orthographic (no perspective distortion), identical scale and height, feet on one shared ground line,
   relaxed symmetrical A-pose (arms angled about 30 degrees away from the body, palms facing the legs, legs slightly apart),
   neutral friendly face. The left and right views are exact mirror profiles of each other.
3. BOTTOM RIGHT: an expression row of 6 head-and-shoulders portraits of the SAME character, front view, same size, left to right:
   happy smile, big laugh with closed curved eyes, surprised with round eyes and small open mouth,
   sleepy with half-closed eyes, sad with a small pout and teary eyes, determined with a confident grin and a wink.
A soft round contact shadow under each full-body figure.

STYLE
A cute stylized human character rendered as a soft 3D toy figure, like a premium vinyl / clay collectible,
matching a cheerful miniature campus diorama game.
Chibi proportion: about 2.5 heads tall. A big round head, a small compact torso, short legs,
short arms whose hands reach only to the hips, slightly oversized rounded shoes.
Round chunky shapes, smooth matte surfaces with a soft subtle subsurface glow, simple clean folds,
no realistic skin texture, no hard outlines, no tiny detail.
Face: big glossy dark brown eyes with one white highlight each, small soft eyebrows, tiny mouth,
soft pink blush cheeks, small simple nose. Expressions are clear and readable but stay cute and soft.
Lighting and color: bright sunny pastel palette, high brightness, gentle saturation,
warm sunlight from the upper left, soft blue-tinted shadows, soft ambient occlusion.
Exactly the same design, colors and proportions in every view and portrait.

OUTFIT (campus adventurer)
- A short-brimmed explorer bucket hat in warm khaki (#C9B48A) with a thin deep crimson band (#A6192E).
- A light cream (#F3EEDD) short-sleeve explorer shirt with two chest pockets, worn open over a deep crimson (#A6192E) T-shirt.
- A small rounded brown leather crossbody satchel on one hip, and a rolled paper map tucked into the strap.
- Knee-length olive-green (#7C8C5A) cargo shorts with side pockets.
- Chunky rounded hiking boots in caramel brown (#A8743F) with cream socks folded over the top.
- Hair (MALE): short soft dark brown hair with a gentle side swept fringe peeking out under the hat.
- Hair (FEMALE): dark brown hair in a short bob or a low ponytail with a crimson hair tie, peeking out under the hat.
- School identity: only one small round deep crimson badge on the satchel flap (blank, no text or symbol).
Keep clothing shapes simple and separate (hat, shirt, shorts, boots clearly divided) so it can be modeled in parts.
Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere.
```

## 프롬프트 1 — 캐릭터 시트 (남 · 여 턴어라운드)

```
Create ONE single image: a professional character turnaround sheet for a mobile game player character,
on a plain flat light cream background (#FAF6EC), horizontal 16:9.
Two characters, a young male and a young female university student, dressed as cheerful campus adventurers.
Top row = the male, bottom row = the female. Each row shows the SAME character in 4 views, left to right:
front view, three-quarter front view, side view (facing right), back view.
All views orthographic (no perspective distortion), same scale, same height, feet on the same ground line,
standing in a relaxed A-pose (arms angled about 30 degrees away from the body, palms facing the legs, legs slightly apart),
neutral friendly face. Evenly spaced, nothing overlapping, a soft round contact shadow under each figure.
No text, labels, numbers, arrows, grid lines or logos.

STYLE
A cute stylized human character rendered as a soft 3D toy figure, like a premium vinyl / clay collectible,
matching a cheerful miniature campus diorama game.
Chibi proportion: about 2.5 heads tall. A big round head, a small compact torso, short legs,
short arms whose hands reach only to the hips, slightly oversized rounded shoes.
Round chunky shapes, smooth matte surfaces with a soft subtle subsurface glow, simple clean folds,
no realistic skin texture, no hard outlines, no tiny detail.
Face: big glossy dark brown eyes with one white highlight each, small soft eyebrows, tiny smiling mouth,
soft pink blush cheeks, small simple nose.
Lighting and color: bright sunny pastel palette, high brightness, gentle saturation,
warm sunlight from the upper left, soft blue-tinted shadows, soft ambient occlusion.
Shapes simple and readable even when very small on a phone map.

OUTFIT (campus adventurer, same design language for both)
- A short-brimmed explorer bucket hat in warm khaki (#C9B48A) with a thin deep crimson band (#A6192E).
- A light cream (#F3EEDD) short-sleeve explorer shirt with two chest pockets, worn open over a deep crimson (#A6192E) T-shirt.
- A small rounded brown leather crossbody satchel on one hip, and a rolled paper map tucked into the strap.
- Male: knee-length olive-green (#7C8C5A) cargo shorts with side pockets.
  Female: olive-green (#7C8C5A) cargo shorts of the same style, hair in a short bob or a low ponytail with a crimson hair tie.
- Chunky rounded hiking boots in caramel brown (#A8743F) with cream socks folded over the top.
- Male hair: short soft dark brown hair with a gentle side swept fringe peeking out under the hat.
- School identity: only one small round deep crimson badge on the satchel flap (blank, no text or symbol).
Keep clothing shapes simple and separate (hat, shirt, shorts, boots clearly divided) so it can be modeled in parts.
Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere.
```

## 프롬프트 2 — 3D 변환용 정면 한 장

프롬프트 1 결과에서 고른 캐릭터를 첨부하고 붙여 넣는다. `[MALE / FEMALE]` 은 하나만 남긴다.

```
Using the attached character sheet as the exact design reference, create ONE single image of only the [MALE / FEMALE] character.
Front view, orthographic, full body centered, standing in a symmetrical A-pose
(arms angled about 30 degrees away from the body, palms facing the legs, legs slightly apart, feet pointing forward),
neutral friendly expression, eyes looking straight ahead.
Plain flat light grey background (#E6E6E6), even soft studio lighting from the front, very soft shadows,
no contact shadow, no floor, nothing else in the image. Square 1:1, the character fills about 85% of the height.
Keep exactly the same proportions, colors, outfit and soft vinyl-toy 3D style as the reference.
No text, labels, logos or watermarks.
```

## 쓰는 팁

- 한 장에 다 안 들어가 그림이 뭉개지면 1-B 를 두 번에 나눈다: LAYOUT 의 1 · 2 만 남긴 "전신 시트" 와, 3 만 남긴 "표정 시트".
- 결과가 사람처럼 길쭉하게 나오면 프롬프트 맨 앞에 `Very chibi, the head is about 40% of the total height.` 를 붙인다.
- 옷 색이 바뀌면 색 코드 줄만 다시 강조해서 "Keep the colors exactly: ..." 로 한 번 더 요청한다.
- 정면 한 장 (프롬프트 2) 은 AI 이미지→3D 에 넣기 좋고, 시트 (프롬프트 1) 는 블렌더에서 옆 · 뒤 모양을 맞출 때 본다.
